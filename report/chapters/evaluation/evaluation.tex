\section{Evaluation}
As the project comes to an end it is important to evaluate the product in relation to the research questions presented in the
beginning of the paper. RQ1 revolves around the quality of levels produced by an algorithm. Specifically, it mentions the ability to create
good player flow by balancing level difficulty with player skill. RQ2 aims to compare GA and RL in terms of player experience, that once again
refers to balancing level difficulty with player skill. The question also touches on implementation performance differences by player engagement,
as slow level generation can reduce player engagement, causing them to break out of the flow state.

To evaluate the research question a benchmark test was created for the implemented generators. The test focused on the following metrics gathered from
the research questions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Level Generation Speed:} The generators ability to quickly generate a new dungeon level.
  \item \textbf{Level Generation Quality:} The generators ability to generate a new solvable level with a set difficulty target.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Test Design}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Generation Speed Benchmark:} Compare the time required to generate a single level between the RL generator and GA generator.
  \item \textbf{Generation Quality Benchmark:} Compare the solvability rate and difficulty accuracy of the levels created by the RL generator and GA generator.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Test Methodology}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Speed Benchmark:} The speed benchmark consisted of generating 1000 levels and then measuring the total generation time
        and afterwards calculating the average level generation time.
  \item \textbf{Quality Benchmark:} The quality benchmark used the previously generated 1000 levels. Each level was played by the
        deterministic agent from Iteration 2. The agents results were recorded and from those the ratio of solvable to unsolvable levels and
        the difference between the agents reward and the assigned level difficulty was calculated.
\end{enumerate}
